# Glossary: the words of Tenun in Bahasa Indonesia

One Indonesian word for each product term, used the same way on every screen. The words are the ones
Indonesian Excel and Power BI users already meet (Microsoft's own Indonesian terms where they exist), or the
English word where that is what people say at work ("filter", "target", "KPI").

When a text in `id.json` names one of these things, it uses the word below. Do not change a word for variety.

## Style

- Formal but friendly, short. Write without "Anda" where the sentence works without it ("Simpan perubahan?",
  not "Apakah Anda ingin menyimpan perubahan?").
- Buttons and menu items are verbs in the base form: "Simpan", "Tambah petak", "Buka".
- Keep every `{placeholder}`, number, code, key name and product name as it is.
- Sentence case, as in English: only the first word starts with a capital.
