Weave your spreadsheets and databases into one dashboard.

Tenun is a free dashboard app that runs on your computer. Draw a line between an Excel column and a database column, and every chart can use both.

Download View on GitHub

Windows, macOS and Linux · No server, no Docker, no account · MIT or Apache-2.0

Join sources, not just tables

Sales in Excel, stores in a CSV export, customers in PostgreSQL. Drag from a column to a column, and Tenun writes the joins. It suggests the likely links and checks them against the data.

Click to filter everything

Click a bar, a share or a row. Every other chart filters to it, even when its data comes from another source. Add a filter tile for a list, a dropdown or a date range.

Live from your files

Save the Excel file and the dashboard updates. Databases refresh on an interval you choose. Nothing is copied to a server.

Honest numbers

Half of October is compared with the first half of September, not with all of it. Unfinished periods are drawn dashed and labelled. Pies and donuts only for parts of a whole, six slices at most. No gauges.

Good-looking by default

No colour pickers, no font menus. A fixed palette checked for colour blindness, in light and dark mode. You cannot make it ugly.

Export one HTML file

Send a dashboard, or a whole site, as a single file. It opens offline in any browser, and click-to-filter still works. You can also save a picture or a PDF, or the numbers of one tile as CSV or Excel.

The model is a drawing

Each table is a card. Each relationship is a line. The dot marks the side with unique values, like an ID. If neither side is unique, Tenun refuses the link, because the totals would be counted twice.

One formula, three sources

Sales come from an Excel file, targets from an API, stores from a CSV export. Write SUM([sales].[amount]) / SUM([targets].[target]) once, name it Achievement, and use it in any tile. Tenun adds up each table on its own, so a target is never counted once for each sale. Calculated columns use 64 functions with the names Excel uses.

Down to every kabupaten

Colour Indonesia's 38 provinces or 514 kabupaten and kota, or the countries of the world. Write places the way your data already does: Jawa Barat, Jabar, 32 or Kab. Bandung. Tenun lists the names it could not place. The maps ship with the app and work offline.

Number cards you design

Choose the parts of a card, their order, their size and the background. Add a small chart, a target, or a rule that says "Below target" in words. Save a look as a card style and use it on every dashboard. The colours still come from the fixed palette, so it stays good-looking.

Write the story next to the numbers

Text tiles have headings, lists, quotes and callouts, and numbers that follow the filters: "Revenue is 18.4K in Oct 1–14, ≈ Same against the same days of September." A story page puts text, cards and charts in one column, and prints on A4 pages that never cut a chart.

A website, not a stack of files

Make a workspace a site, with a top bar or a sidebar, groups of pages and a home page. A click on a bar can open another page, kept to that bar. Export the whole site as one HTML file or a folder for any web host, or publish it at one link.

Simple by default. Advanced when you want it.

Tenun starts with the charts an Excel user knows: bars, lines, areas, scatter, histogram, waterfall and pivot tables with totals. Keep the top 5 and group the rest as Other. Show a value as a share, a running total or a change. Add a target and a trend line. Clean a messy sheet, turn months into rows, read a whole folder of files, or read a web link to CSV or JSON data.

One free switch, Advanced features, adds calculated columns, measures and new tables made by append, merge or group. It adds heat maps, funnels, box plots, bullet charts, Sankey, calendar, cohort and Gantt charts, and small multiples. Dashboards that use them still open when the switch is off.

Share it online, keep the data at home

Choose Publish and a dashboard, or a whole site, gets a private link on tenun.cloud. Tenun builds it on your computer and sends only the results. The cloud never connects to your files or databases, and never sees a password.

  • Anyone with the link, or only people you invite.
  • Updates by itself when the data changes, hourly or daily. tenun agent keeps it going on a computer that stays on.
  • Never silently stale: every page says when it was updated, and warns when an update is late.
  • Free for up to 3 dashboards or sites. A site holds up to 10 pages.
  • The Publish button is hidden in this release. It comes back when tenun.cloud opens.
