From a spreadsheet to a dashboard in three steps

  1. 01

    Add your data

    Excel, CSV, Parquet, SQLite, DuckDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL, a folder of files or a web link. Tenun reads them where they are.

  2. 02

    Draw relationships

    Link a column in one source to a column in another. Tenun suggests the likely links and checks them against the data.

  3. 03

    Build the dashboard

    Pick a value and a group. Click any bar to filter every chart. Save the Excel file and the dashboard updates.

Choices

The big tools give you every option. Tenun gives you a good chart.

Power BI, Tableau and Looker Studio can draw anything, so most dashboards end up crowded and hard to read. Tenun leaves some things out on purpose. Each has a better answer.

Tour

Simple to start. Deep when you need it.

Build New in 0.5.0

Model

The model is a drawing

Each table is a card. Each relationship is a line. Drag from a column to a column, and Tenun writes the joins, even across sources. The dot marks the side with unique values, like an ID. If neither side is unique, Tenun refuses the link, because the totals would be counted twice.

Measures

One formula, three sources

Sales come from an Excel file, targets from an API, stores from a CSV export. Write the formula once, name it Achievement, and use it in any tile.

SUM([sales].[amount]) / SUM([targets].[target])

Tenun adds up each table on its own, so a target is never counted once for each sale. Calculated columns use 64 functions with the names Excel uses.

Maps

Down to every kabupaten

Colour Indonesia's 38 provinces or 514 kabupaten and kota, or the countries of the world. Write places the way your data already does:

Jawa Barat Jabar 32 Kab. Bandung

Tenun lists the names it could not place. The maps ship with the app and work offline.

Number cards

Number cards you design

Choose the parts of a card, their order, their size and the background. Add a small chart, a target, or a rule that says "Below target" in words. Save a look as a card style and use it on every dashboard. The colours still come from the fixed palette.

Stories

Write the story next to the numbers

Text tiles have headings, lists, quotes and callouts, and numbers that follow the filters: "Revenue is 18.4K in Oct 1–14, ≈ Same against the same days of September."

A story page puts text, cards and charts in one column, and prints on A4 pages that never cut a chart.

Sites

A website, not a stack of files

Make a workspace a site, with a top bar or a sidebar, groups of pages and a home page. A click on a bar can open another page, kept to that bar.

Export the whole site as one HTML file or a folder for any web host, or publish it at one link. You can also save a picture or a PDF, or the numbers of one tile as CSV or Excel.

Saved views and drill-down · new

Save the way you read it

A saved view remembers the clicks, the filter choices, the time grain and how far each chart is drilled. Keep one for the Monday meeting and one for each region.

Drill down from years to months to days, or from a region to its stores, on the same chart.

What if · new

Ask "what if"

Put a slider on the dashboard and let a measure read it: what if prices rise by 5 %? Every chart follows as you move it, and an exported file keeps each step.

A business year that starts in April, the stock on the last day of a month, a store's share of its region: each is one setting or one function.

Forecast · new

See what comes next, and what stands out

A line over time shows its next months with a 90 % range. Rings mark the months far from the ones around them, with a list under the chart.

Both are worked out on your computer from the chart's own numbers, and say so in words when there are too few.

Alerts · new

Hear about it in your chat

An alert watches a number. When revenue goes below what you set, Tenun sends one message to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram or any web address, and one when it is back.

The app checks while it is open, and tenun agent checks on a computer that stays on. The address stays on your computer.

Your own AI · new

Bring your own AI

Ask the AI app you already use, such as Claude, to build a dashboard. tenun mcp gives it Tenun's tools. Each change shows in the app at once, with Undo.

Tenun calls no AI and sends nothing anywhere. The AI app sends what it reads to its own company. It cannot run SQL, read your files, see a password or publish.

Simple · the default

The charts an Excel user knows

Bars, lines, areas, scatter, histogram, waterfall and pivot tables with totals. Keep the top 5 and group the rest as Other. Show a value as a share, a running total or a change. Add a target and a trend line. Clean a messy sheet, turn months into rows, read a whole folder of files, or read a web link to CSV or JSON data.

Advanced · one free switch

Everything an analyst asks for

Turn on Advanced features in Settings. It adds calculated columns, measures and new tables made by append, merge or group. It adds heat maps, funnels, box plots, bullet charts, Sankey, calendar, cohort and Gantt charts, and small multiples. Version 0.5.0 adds drill-down, saved views, parameters, forecasts, unusual values and alerts. Dashboards that use them still open when the switch is off.

tenun.cloud

Share it online, keep the data at home

Choose Publish and a dashboard, or a whole site, gets a private link on tenun.cloud. Tenun builds it on your computer and sends only the results. The cloud never connects to your files or databases, and never sees a password.

The Publish button is hidden in this release. It comes back when tenun.cloud opens.

  • Who can see itAnyone with the link, anyone with the link and a password, or only people you invite. Put a dashboard inside your own website with one line of code.
  • Updates by itselfWhen the data changes, hourly or daily. tenun agent keeps it going on a computer that stays on.
  • Never silently staleEvery page says when it was updated, and warns when an update is late.
  • FreeFor up to 3 dashboards or sites. A site holds up to 10 pages.
