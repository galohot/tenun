Why Tenun

A fresh, calm alternative to Power BI, Tableau and Looker Studio.

Tenun does the everyday part of their work: join your sources, draw charts that filter each other, and share the result. It does it on your own computer, for free.

Features

Join sources, not just tables

Sales in Excel, stores in a CSV export, customers in PostgreSQL. Drag from a column to a column, and Tenun writes the joins. It suggests the likely links and checks them against the data.

Click to filter everything

Click a bar, a share or a row. Every other chart filters to it, even when its data comes from another source. Add a filter tile for a list, a dropdown or a date range.

Live from your files

Save the Excel file and the dashboard updates. Databases refresh on an interval you choose. Nothing is copied to a server.

Honest numbers

Half of October is compared with the first half of September, not with all of it. Unfinished periods are drawn dashed and labelled. Pies and donuts only for parts of a whole, six slices at most. No gauges.

Good-looking by default

No colour pickers, no font menus. A fixed palette checked for colour blindness, in light and dark mode. You cannot make it ugly.

Export one HTML file

Send a dashboard, or a whole site, as a single file. It opens offline in any browser, and click-to-filter still works. You can also save a picture or a PDF, or the numbers of one tile as CSV or Excel.

Model

The model is a drawing

Each table is a card. Each relationship is a line. The dot marks the side with unique values, like an ID. If neither side is unique, Tenun refuses the link, because the totals would be counted twice.

Measures

One formula, three sources

Sales come from an Excel file, targets from an API, stores from a CSV export. Write the formula once, name it Achievement, and use it in any tile.

SUM([sales].[amount]) / SUM([targets].[target])

Tenun adds up each table on its own, so a target is never counted once for each sale. Calculated columns use 64 functions with the names Excel uses.

Maps

Down to every kabupaten

Colour Indonesia's 38 provinces or 514 kabupaten and kota, or the countries of the world. Write places the way your data already does:

Jawa Barat Jabar 32 Kab. Bandung

Tenun lists the names it could not place. The maps ship with the app and work offline.

Number cards

Number cards you design

Choose the parts of a card, their order, their size and the background. Add a small chart, a target, or a rule that says "Below target" in words. Save a look as a card style and use it on every dashboard. The colours still come from the fixed palette, so it stays good-looking.

Stories

Write the story next to the numbers

Text tiles have headings, lists, quotes and callouts, and numbers that follow the filters: "Revenue is 18.4K in Oct 1–14, ≈ Same against the same days of September." A story page puts text, cards and charts in one column, and prints on A4 pages that never cut a chart.

Sites

A website, not a stack of files

Make a workspace a site, with a top bar or a sidebar, groups of pages and a home page. A click on a bar can open another page, kept to that bar. Export the whole site as one HTML file or a folder for any web host, or publish it at one link.

Saved views and drill-down

Save the way you read it

A saved view remembers the clicks, the filter choices, the time grain and how far each chart is drilled. Keep one for the Monday meeting and one for each region. Drill down from years to months to days, or from a region to its stores, on the same chart.

What if

Ask "what if"

Put a slider on the dashboard and let a measure read it: what if prices rise by 5 %? Every chart follows as you move it, and an exported file keeps each step.

A business year that starts in April, the stock on the last day of a month, a store's share of its region: each is one setting or one function.

Forecast

See what comes next, and what stands out

A line over time shows its next months with a 90 % range. Rings mark the months far from the ones around them, with a list under the chart. Both are worked out on your computer from the chart's own numbers, and say so in words when there are too few.

Alerts

Hear about it in your chat

An alert watches a number. When revenue goes below what you set, Tenun sends one message to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram or any web address, and one when it is back.

The app checks while it is open, and tenun agent checks on a computer that stays on. The address stays on your computer.

Your own AI

Bring your own AI

Ask the AI app you already use, such as Claude, to build a dashboard. tenun mcp gives it Tenun's tools. Each change shows in the app at once, with Undo.

Tenun calls no AI and sends nothing anywhere. The AI app sends what it reads to its own company. It cannot run SQL, read your files, see a password or publish.

Advanced features · one free switch

Simple by default. Advanced when you want it.

Simple

Tenun starts with the charts an Excel user knows: bars, lines, areas, scatter, histogram, waterfall and pivot tables with totals. Keep the top 5 and group the rest as Other. Show a value as a share, a running total or a change. Add a target and a trend line. Clean a messy sheet, turn months into rows, read a whole folder of files, or read a web link to CSV or JSON data.

Advanced

Turn on Advanced features in Settings. It adds calculated columns, measures and new tables made by append, merge or group. It adds heat maps, funnels, box plots, bullet charts, Sankey, calendar, cohort and Gantt charts, and small multiples. Version 0.5.0 adds drill-down, views you save, parameters, forecasts, unusual values and alerts. Dashboards that use them still open when the switch is off.

What Tenun does not do, on purpose

Tenun aims to do a few things well. These are left out by choice. Each has an answer.

  • GaugesA number tile with a target.
  • Dual-axis chartsSeparate panels, or indexed lines, so each value keeps an honest axis.
  • Treemaps and packed bubblesA bar chart with the top 10 and Other.
  • Pareto chartsA sorted bar chart with a running total.
  • Radar, 3D, ribbon and word cloud chartsA bar, line or table.
  • Custom colours, themes and fontsThe fixed palette, checked for colour blindness in light and dark mode.
  • Free-floating layoutThe grid. Tiles snap into place and never overlap.
  • Third-party, R or Python visualsThe built-in charts. Everything is computed in one place, so every export agrees.
  • Roles and governanceYour own files and your own permissions. Tenun runs on your computer.

tenun.cloud

Share it online, keep the data at home

Choose Publish and a dashboard, or a whole site, gets a private link on tenun.cloud. Tenun builds it on your computer and sends only the results. The cloud never connects to your files or databases, and never sees a password.

The Publish button is hidden in this release. It comes back when tenun.cloud opens.

  • Anyone with the link, anyone with the link and a password, or only people you invite. Put a dashboard inside your own website with one line of code.
  • Updates by itself when the data changes, hourly or daily. tenun agent keeps it going on a computer that stays on.
  • Never silently stale: every page says when it was updated, and warns when an update is late.
  • Free for up to 3 dashboards or sites. A site holds up to 10 pages.
