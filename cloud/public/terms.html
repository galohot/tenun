tenun.cloud is a free service of the Tenun open-source project. Using it means you accept these rules.

What you may publish

  • Only data you have the right to share with the people who will see it.
  • No personal data of other people unless you have their consent or another legal basis.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing meant to deceive or harm.

The free plan

Up to 3 published dashboards, up to 10 MB each, with a limit on how often they update. The limits are shown in the app and may change.

No guarantee

The service is provided as it is, without any warranty. It may be unavailable, and it may change or end. Keep your own copies: your data always stays in your files and databases.

Removal

We may remove dashboards that break these rules or the law.
