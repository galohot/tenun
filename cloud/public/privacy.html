Privacy

tenun.cloud stores only what is needed to show the dashboards you publish. The Tenun app on your computer works without it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and name, from Google sign-in.
  • Published dashboards: the title, the layout, and the results each tile shows (for example "Amount by store"), including the results needed to filter by clicking. Not your files, not your databases, and not your database passwords: those never leave your computer.
  • Freshness: when each snapshot was made, the name of the computer that published it (you choose this label), and how often it updates.
  • Connected apps: a fingerprint (hash) of each publishing key, when it was created and last used. The key itself is not stored.
  • Invited people: the email addresses you add to a dashboard.
  • Passwords of dashboards: a hash of each password (a form that cannot be turned back into the password), never the password itself.
  • Email updates: the addresses that get the updates of a dashboard (its owner, and invited people who ask for them), a random link that stops them, and when the last one went. An email holds the dashboard's numbers as its owner's computer wrote them.
  • Wrong passwords: when someone types a wrong password, a hash of their internet address and the time, to allow at most five wrong tries a minute. Deleted within a day.

Who can see it

  • A dashboard set to anyone with the link can be seen by anyone who has its address. The address is long and random, and search engines are told not to index it.
  • A dashboard set to anyone with the password can be seen by anyone who has its address and its password.
  • A dashboard set to invited people can be seen only after signing in with an email you invited.
  • A dashboard can be shown inside other websites only when its owner allows it. A dashboard for invited people never can.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

  • The last five versions of each dashboard. Older versions are deleted when a new one arrives.
  • Deleting a dashboard deletes every version at once. Deleting your account deletes everything listed above.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. It is needed for sign-in and is not used for anything else.

After a right password, one cookie for that dashboard keeps it open in that browser for 7 days. It holds no password and is not used for anything else.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare stores and serves the data.
  • WorkOS and Google handle sign-in.

Your choices

You can see, disconnect and delete everything on your account page. For anything else, open an issue on GitHub.
